## Resetting a password

On the sign-in page, choose Forgot password and enter the email address used for
the account. If an account matches, we send a password-reset link. For privacy,
the page does not reveal whether an email address belongs to an account. Check
spam or junk folders if the message does not arrive.

The link expires 30 minutes after it is issued and can be used once. If it has
expired or already been used, request a new link. A new request may invalidate a
previous link. Support cannot view, choose, or send a customer's current
password.

## Lost access to the account email

If the customer no longer controls the email address on the account, use the
account-recovery form and follow the identity-verification instructions. Support
may request information needed to verify ownership but will never ask for a
password, full card number, security code, or one-time sign-in code. Recovery is
not guaranteed until verification is complete.

## Profile and order security

Order history, addresses, payment details, cancellation status, and tracking
information are private account data. A general policy assistant cannot access
or disclose them. Sign in to view the account, or contact support through the
verified account-recovery channel if sign-in is unavailable.

Do not send authentication codes to anyone who contacts you unexpectedly. If
you suspect unauthorized access, reset the password from a trusted device,
secure the associated email account, and contact support. Support can explain
the process but cannot verify a particular login or change account details
without identity checks.
