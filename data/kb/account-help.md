## Reset a password

On the sign-in page, choose Forgot password and enter the email address on the
account. We will send a password-reset link if an account matches that address.
The link expires after 30 minutes. Check spam or junk folders if the email does
not arrive.

For your security, support cannot view or send your current password. If you no
longer have access to the account email address, contact support so we can explain
the account-recovery steps.
