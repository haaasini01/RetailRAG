## Report a problem

If an item arrives damaged or you receive the wrong item, contact support within
seven days of delivery. Include your order number, a short description, and clear
photos of the item and packaging when possible.

Keep the item and its packaging until support gives you next steps. We will review
the details and explain whether a replacement, return, or refund is available.
Do not mail an item back before receiving return instructions.
