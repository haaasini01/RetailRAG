## Damage on arrival or wrong item

Report visible shipping damage or an incorrect item within five calendar days
after confirmed delivery. Include the order number, the affected product, a short
description, and clear photos of the item, shipping label, and packaging when
possible. Do not include full payment-card details or account passwords.

Keep the item, its packaging, and all included parts while support reviews the
report. Do not mail it back before receiving written return instructions. After
review, support may authorize a replacement, a return for refund, or another
resolution permitted by the applicable policy. A replacement depends on stock;
this policy does not promise that a particular color or model is available.

## Defects discovered after delivery

If a defect is not reasonably visible at delivery, report it within five calendar
days after discovery and no later than 30 calendar days after delivery. This
short reporting path helps us investigate the shipment; it does not shorten a
separate valid warranty period. For a product-specific repair or replacement
claim after that period, see **Product warranty**.

The five-day visible-damage reporting period and the 30-day hidden-defect outer
limit are different from the change-of-mind return window. A customer who wants
to return an otherwise conforming item must still meet **Returns, exclusions,
and refunds** deadlines and condition requirements.

## Shipping errors and label fees

When support confirms that Pine & Peak sent the wrong item or an item arrived
damaged, the $7.95 standard change-of-mind return-label charge is waived. Do not
select a paid return label before support reviews the issue. If a return is
approved, follow the instructions in the case rather than the ordinary
change-of-mind flow.

An item that appears damaged because of use, accidental impact after delivery, or
an unauthorized modification is not automatically a shipping-damage claim. It
may still qualify for the limited coverage in **Product warranty** if the
reported failure and product meet that policy's conditions.
