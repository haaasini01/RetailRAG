## Redeeming a gift card

Enter the gift-card code in the Gift card field during checkout and select Apply.
The available balance is deducted from the order total. If the order costs more
than the gift-card balance, pay the remainder with another accepted payment
method.

## Lost or unused cards

Treat gift-card codes like cash and do not post them publicly. Contact support
with the purchase receipt if a card you bought is missing or its balance appears
incorrect. Gift cards cannot be exchanged for cash except where required by law.
