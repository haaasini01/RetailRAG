## Redeeming a gift card

Enter the gift-card code in the Gift card field at checkout and select Apply.
The available balance is applied to the order total. If the balance is lower
than the total, pay the remainder with one accepted credit or debit card. If the
balance exceeds the total, the unused amount remains available on the card.

Gift cards count as a payment method, not merchandise, and do not count toward
the $75 free-standard-shipping merchandise threshold. Gift cards cannot be used
to buy another gift card. A card already applied to a submitted order cannot be
removed or transferred to another order through self-service.

## Expiration, cash, and lost codes

Purchased Pine & Peak gift cards do not expire. They are not redeemable for cash
except where applicable law requires it. Treat the code like cash; anyone who
obtains the code may be able to use its balance. Do not post it publicly or send
it in an unsecured message.

If a purchased card is missing or its balance appears wrong, contact support with
the purchase receipt and enough information to identify the card securely. We
cannot promise replacement without verifying the purchase and remaining balance.
The assistant cannot look up a card balance or recover a code.

## Promotional credits

Promotional credits are distinct from purchased gift cards. Their expiration
date, eligible products, and minimum-spend conditions are stated in the offer
that issued them. Unless the offer explicitly allows otherwise, promotional
credits cannot be combined with another promotional credit, converted to cash,
or applied to taxes or shipping.

If a purchase paid partly with a promotional credit is refunded, the original
offer terms determine whether that credit is restored and whether it is still
valid. A return does not extend an expired promotional credit. For refund
destination of a purchased gift card, see **Payment methods, authorizations, and
refunds**.
