title: Cancelling or changing an order
category: orders
---
## Before an order ships

You can request a cancellation from Order history while the order is still marked
Processing. Select the order and choose Cancel order. If that option is no longer
available, the warehouse has started preparing the parcel and we cannot guarantee
a change or cancellation.

## After dispatch

An order cannot be edited after dispatch. Once it is delivered, eligible items may
be returned under the Returns and refunds policy. Do not refuse a parcel as a
substitute for starting a return, because carrier return handling can delay
resolution.
