## Find your tracking link

When an order leaves our warehouse, we email a dispatch confirmation with a
carrier tracking link. You can also sign in, open Order history, and select the
order to view its status.

Tracking may not show movement immediately. The carrier usually needs to scan the
parcel before its tracking page displays an estimated delivery date. Allow up to
one business day after dispatch for the first scan.

If there is no tracking update after three business days, contact StoreGuide
support with your order number. Do not send full payment-card details.
