## Finding tracking details

After a parcel leaves the warehouse, we email a dispatch confirmation with its
carrier tracking link. Customers can also sign in, open Order history, and select
the order. Each parcel in a split shipment has its own tracking number. A policy
assistant cannot retrieve an individual tracking number or see current carrier
events without an order lookup.

## First scan and in-transit updates

The carrier may take up to one business day after dispatch to record the first
scan. Until that scan appears, the tracking page may show only that shipment
information was received; this does not by itself mean the parcel is lost.
Business days are Monday through Friday, excluding U.S. federal holidays.

If there is no carrier movement for three full business days after the first
scan, contact support with the order number. A shipping estimate is not a
guaranteed delivery appointment. Weather, carrier capacity, address problems, and
other events can delay scans or delivery.

## Marked delivered but not found

Check the delivery location, household members, building reception, and any
carrier-provided photo or location note. Allow one business day after the
delivered scan in case the parcel was scanned early or left nearby. If it is
still missing, contact support with the order number for an investigation.

Do not open a charge dispute solely because the tracking page has not updated;
contact support first so the delivery can be investigated. Never send a full
payment-card number or account password in a support message.

## Delivery estimates and shipping policy

Warehouse processing time is separate from transit time. Standard transit is
usually four to six business days after carrier pickup; expedited transit is
usually two business days when offered. For processing cutoffs, destinations,
shipping prices, and estimates, see **Shipping, delivery estimates, and
charges**.
