## Accepted payment methods

Checkout accepts Visa, Mastercard, and American Express credit or debit cards.
Available payment options are displayed before you place the order. StoreGuide
gift cards can be applied at checkout, and any remaining balance must be paid
with another displayed method.

## Pending charges

Your bank may show a temporary authorization when you place an order. This is a
pending hold, not a second completed charge. The bank releases it according to
its own processing schedule if an order is cancelled or the final amount changes.

For a completed charge you do not recognize, contact support with the order
number and amount. Never include your full card number, security code, or banking
password in a support message.
