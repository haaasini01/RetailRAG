## Accepted payment methods

Checkout accepts Visa, Mastercard, and American Express credit or debit cards,
plus Pine & Peak Market gift cards. Available options are displayed before the
customer places an order. A gift card may be combined with one accepted card for
the remaining balance; multiple bank cards cannot be combined in one checkout.
Payment methods cannot be changed after order submission.

## Authorization and capture

When an order is submitted, the bank may show a temporary authorization. An
authorization reserves funds but is not a second completed charge. We normally
capture payment when each parcel is prepared for dispatch; a split shipment may
therefore produce separate completed charges for the shipped portions. The total
captured will not exceed the final order total, apart from separately disclosed
charges.

If an order is cancelled before dispatch, we void the authorization or refund any
captured amount. The bank controls when a pending authorization disappears; it
may remain visible for several business days after we release it. We cannot
promise the exact release date.

## Discounts, tax, and final amount

The amount shown at checkout includes applied discounts, estimated tax, and
selected shipping. The final tax may be recalculated where required. A discount
does not reduce a separately stated shipping or oversized-item fee unless the
offer terms say it does. Promotion eligibility is determined by the offer's
stated conditions and cannot be inferred from a discount code alone.

Pine & Peak Market does not match competitor prices. A competitor's listed
price, coupon, or post-purchase price change does not change the amount paid on
an existing order.

## Refund destinations and timing

Approved refunds go to the original payment method. Amounts paid by gift card
return to a gift card; any card-paid portion returns to the original card. After
we issue a refund, allow five to ten business days for the provider to post it.
This is a provider-dependent estimate, not a guaranteed posting date.

For return eligibility and return-label deductions, see **Returns, exclusions,
and refunds**. This policy does not let support view a customer's card account,
confirm whether a charge settled, or identify an order without an account lookup.
Never provide a full card number, security code, banking password, or one-time
authentication code in a support request.
