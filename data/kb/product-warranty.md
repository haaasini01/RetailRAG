## Covered products and warranty periods

Pine & Peak Market-branded electronic products have a limited warranty against
defects in materials and workmanship for 24 months from confirmed delivery.
Pine & Peak-branded non-electronic accessories have 12 months of coverage.
Products made by another manufacturer are covered only by the warranty period
and terms published for that product or included with it; this policy does not
extend a third-party manufacturer's warranty.

Coverage applies to the original retail purchaser and requires proof of purchase.
The warranty period is measured from delivery, not order placement or the date a
claim is opened. Gift recipients may need the original purchaser's receipt.
Consumables and products marked Final sale are not covered by this voluntary
limited warranty unless their product listing expressly says otherwise. Rights
provided by applicable law are not reduced by this policy.

## What is and is not covered

An eligible claim must describe a failure caused by a materials or workmanship
defect during the applicable coverage period. Coverage does not include ordinary
wear, cosmetic changes that do not affect function, accidental damage, misuse,
failure to follow care instructions, use outside the product's intended purpose,
unauthorized repair or modification, or commercial use.

A product damaged in transit should be reported under **Damaged, defective, or
incorrect deliveries**. A transit-damage report and a warranty claim have
different reporting windows. A late shipping-damage report is not automatically
a warranty denial if a separate covered defect is discovered during the
warranty period.

## Starting a claim and possible outcomes

Start a claim through support with proof of purchase, product model, a
description of the failure, and photos or a short video if requested. Do not
mail a product before receiving a case number and written instructions. Support
may ask reasonable diagnostic questions before determining whether the reported
issue is covered.

For an approved claim, Pine & Peak may repair the product, replace it with the
same or a functionally equivalent product, or refund the amount paid, as allowed
by the product terms and applicable law. A replacement may be refurbished where
permitted and will not restart or extend the original warranty period unless
applicable law requires otherwise. Approval, remedy, and timing are determined
after review; submitting a claim does not guarantee a replacement or refund.

This policy cannot verify an individual purchase, coverage start date, claim
status, or eligibility without an account and product lookup.
