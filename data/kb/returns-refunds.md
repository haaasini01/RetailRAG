## Standard return eligibility

Most physical items may be returned if the return is requested within 30 calendar
days after confirmed delivery. The request must be submitted by the deadline, and
the parcel must be handed to the carrier within 14 calendar days after the return
label is issued. Calendar days include weekends and holidays. If the carrier
tracking proves the parcel was handed over on time, a later transit delay does not
make the return late.

Items must be in resalable condition: unused, unwashed, undamaged, and returned
with all included parts, accessories, and original packaging. Opening an ordinary
shipping carton does not by itself make an item ineligible. A hygiene-sealed item
whose protective seal has been broken cannot be returned for a change of mind.
Items with missing parts or signs of use may be rejected or receive a reduced
refund after inspection.

To request a return, sign in, open Order history, select the delivered item, and
choose Start a return. Eligibility and available options are assessed for each
item. An account-specific order lookup is required to confirm a particular
purchase; this policy alone cannot confirm whether an individual order qualifies.

## Items excluded from change-of-mind returns

The following are not returnable for a change of mind, even during the 30-day
window:

- Items marked **Final sale** on the product page or order confirmation.
- Personalized or custom-made items.
- Digital downloads after delivery.
- Hygiene-sealed products after the protective seal is broken.
- Gift cards, except where applicable law requires otherwise.

These exclusions do not remove rights that apply when an item is defective,
damaged on arrival, or materially different from its description. Use **Damaged
or incorrect items** for reporting those problems. A change-of-mind return window
does not replace a warranty claim; see **Product warranty** for eligible
manufacturer or Pine & Peak products.

## Holiday extension and bundles

Physical items delivered from November 1 through December 15 may be requested for
return through January 15 of the following year. This holiday extension changes
the request deadline only; the condition rules, 14-day return-shipping deadline,
and final-sale, personalized, digital, and hygiene-seal exclusions still apply.

Items sold as a single bundle or kit must be returned together with every
component for a bundle return. Individual items separately listed on one order
may be considered separately. If a promotion required buying multiple items
together, the refund is recalculated using the promotion terms for the items kept;
the refund will not exceed the amount actually paid for the returned item(s).

## Return shipping and refund

For a change-of-mind return, the standard return-label charge is $7.95 and is
deducted from the refund. Oversized-item labels may cost more; the exact amount
is shown before the customer confirms the return. The label charge is waived
when the return is because Pine & Peak sent the wrong item or the item arrived
damaged or defective, once support confirms the issue.

After the return reaches us and passes inspection, we issue the eligible refund to
the original payment method. Refunds normally appear within five to ten business
days after issuance; the payment provider controls the final posting time.
Returned items originally paid for with a gift card are refunded to a gift card.
Shipping charges originally paid are not refunded for a change-of-mind return.

## Orders outside the return period

An item requested after its applicable return deadline is not eligible for a
change-of-mind return. A late report of damage, a wrong item, or a possible
warranty defect is handled under the relevant separate policy, not automatically
as a late change-of-mind return. The assistant cannot inspect an order or grant an
exception; contact support for an individual review.
