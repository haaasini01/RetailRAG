## Return window and condition

Most items can be returned within 30 days of delivery. Items must be unused,
unwashed, and returned with their original packaging and included parts. To start
a return, sign in, open Order history, select the item, and choose Start a return.

Personalized items, digital downloads, and items marked Final sale cannot be
returned unless they arrived damaged or incorrect.

## Refund timing

After an approved return reaches us and passes inspection, we issue the refund to
the original payment method. Allow five to ten business days for the payment
provider to post the refund. The time can vary by provider.

The return instructions shown during the return request include any applicable
return-shipping charge. Contact support if the return option is unavailable for
an item you believe is eligible.
