## Where we ship

Pine & Peak Market ships to street addresses and carrier-supported post-office
boxes in the 48 contiguous United States. We do not currently ship to Alaska,
Hawaii, U.S. territories, APO/FPO/DPO addresses, or destinations outside the
United States. A carrier may reject a post-office box for a particular service;
if so, checkout will not offer that service for the address.

The delivery address cannot be changed after an order is submitted. See
**Cancelling or changing an order** for the limited pre-fulfillment cancellation
window. A forwarding request made directly to a carrier is outside our control
and may affect delivery estimates.

## Processing and delivery estimates

Orders are usually prepared within one to three business days after payment
authorization. A business day is Monday through Friday, excluding U.S. federal
holidays. Orders placed after 2:00 p.m. Eastern Time begin processing on the next
business day. The processing estimate is separate from carrier transit time.

Standard transit is usually four to six business days after carrier pickup.
Expedited transit is usually two business days after pickup when that option is
offered at checkout. Expedited shipping does not skip warehouse processing and is
not a guaranteed delivery date. The checkout estimate is the best estimate for
that order; carrier delays, weather, and address issues can extend it.

An order may arrive in more than one parcel. Each parcel can have a separate
tracking link and delivery date. The shipping threshold is calculated once for
the merchandise in the order; splitting the shipment does not create an
additional shipping charge.

## Charges and free-shipping threshold

Standard shipping costs $6.95 per order. An order qualifies for free standard
shipping when its merchandise subtotal is at least $75 after discounts and
before tax. Gift cards, taxes, shipping charges, and separately stated fees do
not count toward the threshold. Multiple orders cannot be combined to qualify.

Expedited shipping is charged at the amount shown at checkout and is never made
free by the standard-shipping threshold. If an order qualifies for free standard
shipping, choosing expedited service means paying the displayed expedited charge.
We do not add a shipping charge after checkout solely because a later return
reduces the value of the items kept.

## Delays and delivery problems

Carrier estimates are not guarantees. For a parcel marked delivered but not
found, first check the delivery location, household members, building office, and
carrier tracking details. If it is still missing after one business day, contact
support with the order number. Do not send payment-card details. Tracking delays
before the first carrier scan are covered by **Tracking an order**.
