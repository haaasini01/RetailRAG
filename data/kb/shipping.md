## Processing and delivery times

Most orders are prepared within one to two business days. Standard delivery within
the contiguous United States usually takes three to five business days after the
carrier collects the parcel. Business days exclude weekends and store holidays.

## Shipping charges

Standard shipping costs $5.95 per order. Orders with a merchandise subtotal of
$50 or more before tax qualify for free standard shipping. Express shipping, when
available at checkout, has a separate price shown before payment.

We currently ship to addresses in the contiguous United States only. We do not
deliver to P.O. boxes using express service.
